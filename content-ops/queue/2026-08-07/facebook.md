Take a photo of a receipt and your inventory fills itself in.

Not "type it in faster" — you don't type at all. You point the camera at the paper and a list appears: each product, how many, what each one cost, what kind of thing it is, the reference code, the shop and the date.

The bit that matters: the AI doing the reading runs **on your own machine**. The photo doesn't go to anyone's cloud, because there isn't one.

And it doesn't add anything by itself. It leaves you a short list to approve with one tap, and you can fix a price before you accept it — because a small model reading a crumpled receipt gets a line wrong now and then, and you should be the one who catches it.

Two more things it does without being asked: photograph the same receipt again next week and nothing duplicates, and if what you bought is electronics, an appliance or a tool, it works out when your legal warranty ends and warns you before it does.
