Take a photo of a receipt. Your inventory fills itself in.

Not "type it faster" — you don't type at all. Point the camera at the paper and a list appears: each product, how many, what each cost, what kind of thing it is, the reference, the shop and the date.

The bit that matters: the AI doing the reading runs on your own machine at home. The photo doesn't go to anyone's cloud, because there isn't one.

And it doesn't add anything by itself. It leaves you a short list to approve with one tap, and you can fix a price first — because a small model reading a crumpled receipt gets a line wrong sometimes, and you should be the one who catches it.

Two more things, unasked: shoot the same receipt again next week and nothing duplicates, and if it's electronics, an appliance or a tool, it works out when your warranty ends and warns you before it does.
