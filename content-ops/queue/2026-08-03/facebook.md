Your money, in three views — and you only ever use the one that's yours.

Open the money side of your Micelclaw server and the first thing you pick isn't a report. It's who you are today.

**Personal** is for the question everyone has and almost nobody can answer on the spot: am I better off than I was in January, and where is it going? You get your net worth, what came in, what went out, and how much of it you actually kept — with a ring showing what you spend it on, and your cash in and out month by month.

**Invoicing** is for whoever sends invoices. What you've billed, what's actually arrived in the bank, what's owed to you, and what's late — four numbers that people confuse constantly, and the difference between the first two is why businesses that are "doing well" still run out of money.

**Accounting** is the one you open twice a year, when someone asks you for something official.

Same information underneath, three doors in, and the door you don't need stays shut.
