Your money, in three views. You only ever open the one that's yours.

Personal: am I better off than in January, and where is it going? Net worth, what came in, what went out, and how much you actually kept — with a ring of what you spend it on.

Invoicing: what you've billed, what's arrived, what's owed to you, and what's late. Four numbers people confuse all the time, and the gap between the first two is why businesses that look fine still run out of money.

Accounting: the one you open twice a year, when someone asks for something official.

Same information underneath. Three doors in. The one you don't need stays shut. All of it on your own server, at home.
