*One box, several companies.*

A detail from yesterday's post that's easy to miss: the little dropdown next to the module name.

If you run more than one thing — a company plus your own freelance work, or two businesses — they both live on the same server, one click apart. Separate books, separate invoices, separate customers, separate tax numbers. Nothing leaks between them.

You don't need a second server for it, and you don't pay twice. You click the name and everything on screen becomes the other company's.

The same switch is there whether you're looking at your personal spending, your unpaid invoices or the formal books — so "which business am I looking at?" is always answered in the top left corner, not by remembering which tab you had open.
