Small thing in our money module that I like more than it deserves.

Every headline figure prints its own arithmetic underneath. The result for the period isn't a lone number — it reads "29.222,33 € invoiced − 7541,98 € expenses". The VAT card reads "charged 5113,30 € − paid 1112,48 €".

Costs nothing to add. Removes the entire class of "where does that come from?"
