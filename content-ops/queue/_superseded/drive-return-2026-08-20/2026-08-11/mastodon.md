Duplicate finders usually compare filenames, which is why they miss the copies that matter.

Ours groups by what's inside the file. One photo, three locations: two share a name, the third is in the camera roll as beach-sunset-01.jpg. Nothing name-based was going to link them.

It reports and stops — each copy has its own "Keep this".

Honest limit: exact matches only. Re-export the same picture smaller and it's a different file.
