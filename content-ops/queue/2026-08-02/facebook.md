Your assistant has a limited amount of room per conversation — and it isn't only your messages competing for it. Everything the assistant is allowed to do has to be explained to it first, and those explanations take up space too.

One of ours ran out. So we went through the explanations and deleted the parts that were being repeated hundreds of times and the parts describing things the assistant never has to fill in itself. It got 45% lighter without losing a single ability.

There's now a bar in your dashboard showing how full each assistant is and which apps are the expensive ones, so you can decide what it carries every day and what it only picks up when a job needs it.
