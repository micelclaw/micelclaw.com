Your assistant has a limited amount of room per conversation — and it isn't only your messages competing for it. Everything it's allowed to do has to be explained to it first, and those explanations take space too.

One of ours ran out of room. We deleted the explanations that were being repeated hundreds of times and the ones describing things it never fills in itself: 45% lighter, not a single ability lost.

Your dashboard now shows how full each assistant is, and which apps cost the most.
