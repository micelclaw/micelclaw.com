One thing people don't expect from the AI on their own server: it can ask a colleague for help.

If you ask for something that isn't its job — turning a note into audio, digging through your photos, checking a bill — the assistant you're talking to doesn't try to muddle through. It hands the job to the specialist that actually has that ability, waits, and comes back to you with the result in its own words.

Each specialist only reaches the part of your data you allowed it to, and everything they do is written down as if you'd done it yourself. It's your house; they just have different keys.
