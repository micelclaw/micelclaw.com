Ask your server's assistant for something that isn't its job, and it doesn't muddle through — it hands the work to the specialist that actually has that ability, then comes back with the result.

Turning a note into audio, finding that photo, checking a bill. Different jobs, different helpers, and each one only reaches the part of your data you allowed.

Everything they do is written down as if you'd done it yourself.
