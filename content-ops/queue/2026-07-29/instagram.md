Your home server doesn't give you one AI assistant. It gives you a team.

One coordinator that takes what you ask for and knows who should do it, plus six specialists: your files, your photos and voice, your money, your network, your smart home. Each one only reaches the part of your life it was given, and every action is recorded as if you'd done it yourself.

Making them hand work to each other properly took us four months. The write-up is on the blog — including the bug we still haven't fixed.
