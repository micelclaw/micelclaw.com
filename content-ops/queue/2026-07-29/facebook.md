Instead of one assistant that does everything badly, your Micelclaw server gives you a small team. One of them takes your request and knows who should handle it; the others are specialists — your files and documents, your photos and voice, your money, your smart home. Each one only has access to the part of your data it needs, and everything they do is recorded as if you'd done it yourself.

Getting them to work together turned out to be the hard part. For a while, when you asked for something that needed a specialist, the request would quietly go nowhere — no error, no warning, just silence. We've spent four months finding every one of those gaps and writing down what we learned, including the one we haven't fixed yet.
