Everyone who has started an inventory has abandoned it in week two. Not because it isn't useful — because keeping it current costs you now and pays off later.

So we moved the work: photograph a receipt and a vision model running on your own machine reads it, then leaves you a queue of proposed items to approve. It proposes; it doesn't add.

Nothing leaves the house. There's no cloud to send it to.
