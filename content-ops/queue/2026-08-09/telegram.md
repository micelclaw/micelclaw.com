*This week: your money and your things, and why they're better in the same box.*

Two posts went up, both with all the screens.

**Monday — your money, in three views.** Personal, for the question everyone has and nobody can answer on the spot: am I better off than in January, and where is it going? Invoicing, for what you've billed versus what actually arrived, and who's late. Accounting, for the twice a year someone asks you for something official. Same data underneath, three doors in, and the door you don't need stays shut.

**Thursday — photograph a receipt and your inventory fills itself in.** Point the camera at the paper; a model running on your own machine reads it and leaves you a short list to approve. It proposes, it never adds by itself. Shoot the same receipt twice and nothing duplicates. Buy a tool and it works out when your warranty runs out.

The reason they're worth having on the same server: sell something off your own shelf and both sides get written at once. What you earned, and what that thing cost you. So the profit you're looking at is the real one, today, without anyone counting stock.

Both posts also list what's still missing — electronic invoicing, printable labels for items, a proper stocktake mode.
