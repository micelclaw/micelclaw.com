Your money and your things, on the same server — and the moment they meet.

This week we showed both. The money side has three views and you only ever open the one that's yours: personal spending, invoices and who's late, or the formal books for when someone official asks. The things side fills itself in from a photo of a receipt, read by an AI running on your own machine.

Here's why they're better together. Sell something off your own shelf and both sides get written at the same moment: the money you earned, and what those exact items cost you to buy. So the profit you're looking at is the real one, on the day, and nobody had to count anything.

Everything at home, on your own hardware. No cloud, no subscription per user, no company reading your invoices.

Both write-ups on the blog, screens and all — including a straight list of what's still missing.
