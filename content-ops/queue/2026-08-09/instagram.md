Your money and your things, on the same server — and the moment they meet.

The money side has three views, and you only open the one that's yours: personal spending, invoices and who's late, or the formal books for when someone official asks.

The things side fills itself in from a photo of a receipt, read by an AI running on your own machine at home.

Here's why they're better together. Sell something off your own shelf and both get written at the same moment: what you earned, and what those exact items cost you. So the profit you see is the real one, today, and nobody counted anything.

No cloud. No per-user subscription. No company reading your invoices.
