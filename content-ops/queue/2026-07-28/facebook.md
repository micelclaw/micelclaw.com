Your Micelclaw server comes with a private AI assistant that can help with your notes, files, photos and calendar. This week we made sure it can only ever do the things you've allowed — and that every action it takes is recorded, exactly as if you'd done it yourself. Nothing happens behind your back.

We also gave each AI helper its own identity. You can have several, each with its own job, and now each one proves who it is before it does anything — so it can never step outside what it's meant to do. That also fixed a case where a helper you asked for something would quietly do nothing at all.
