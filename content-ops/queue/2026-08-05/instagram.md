Four numbers people mix up constantly — and the reason a business that looks fine still runs out of money.

Invoiced: what you've billed.
Collected: what actually arrived in the bank.
Pending: what's owed to you, still in date.
Overdue: the one to check on a Monday morning.

They are not the same number. Most simple tools only show you the first, so on paper the year looks great while the account is empty.

Your server puts all four side by side the moment you open it, in colour — plus the same again for what you owe, how old your unpaid invoices are, and which customers make up most of your income.

All of it at home, on your own hardware.
