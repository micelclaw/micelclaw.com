Four numbers that people mix up constantly — and the reason a business that looks fine still runs out of money.

**Invoiced** is what you've billed. **Collected** is what actually arrived in the bank. **Pending** is what's owed to you and still in date. **Overdue** is the one to look at on a Monday morning.

They are not the same number, and most simple tools only show you the first one. So on paper the year looks great while the account is empty.

Your Micelclaw server puts all four side by side, in colour, the moment you open it — and the same row again for the other direction: what you've been billed, what you've paid, what you still owe, and what's late. Underneath, how old your unpaid invoices are, and which customers make up most of your income.

No spreadsheets, no chasing. It's on your own hardware and it's just there when you look.
