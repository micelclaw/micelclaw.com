Our inventory scanner asks what you mean before it reads anything.

Open the camera and you pick first: locate it · count it · move it to another bin · check it in or out.

Same barcode, four completely different intentions. A scanner that guesses picks wrong often enough to matter, and undoing a bad stocktake entry is not fun.

Stick a QR on a box, scan, pick Locate, and you see what's inside without opening it.
