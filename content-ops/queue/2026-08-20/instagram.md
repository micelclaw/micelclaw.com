Folders are good at exactly one thing: telling you where you put something.

They can't tell you if you already saved that photo somewhere else. They can't tell you what a document said before you changed it.

So those two answers are on screen now.

One finds the copies — by looking at what's inside the file, not what it's called. The same photo turns up even when one copy is in your camera roll under a name the camera made up. It shows you all of them and waits for you to pick. Nothing gets deleted on its own.

The other is undo, for files. Save over something and the old version is kept automatically. Want what it said three weeks ago? One click.

Everything on your own machine at home.
