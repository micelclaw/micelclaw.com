A folder tells you where a file is. It can't tell you whether you already saved it somewhere else.

Drive has a Duplicates tab that groups by file contents, not names. The clearest case here: the same photo in three places, and the third sits in the camera roll under the name the camera gave it. Nothing name-based was going to find it.

It finds them and stops. Each copy gets its own "Keep this"; nothing moves until you press one.
