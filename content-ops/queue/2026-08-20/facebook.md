Everyone has that moment. You find a file, and then you find another one with almost the same name, and you have no idea which is the good one. So you keep both. And now there are three.

Your files live in folders, and folders are great at one thing: telling you where something is. They can't tell you whether you already saved that photo somewhere else, and they can't tell you what a document said before you changed it.

So we put those two answers on screen.

One shows you the copies. It compares what's actually inside each file, not what it's called — so the same photo shows up even when one copy is sitting in your camera roll under a completely different name. It shows you where they all are, and then it waits. You choose which one to keep; nothing is deleted behind your back.

The other is an undo button for files. Every time you save over something, the previous version is kept automatically. Changed a document three weeks ago and want to see what it said before? It's one click.

All of it on your own machine at home. No cloud, no subscription, no company reading your documents.
