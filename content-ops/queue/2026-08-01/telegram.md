*Why your assistant doesn't carry every tool.*

Every app you give an agent brings a set of actions it can perform, and every one of those actions has to be described to the model before it can use any of them. Describing them is not free: it eats into the space the model has for your actual conversation.

So each app now comes in two sizes. The **core** set is what you need day to day — create, read, list, the obvious things — and it's what the agent carries by default. The **full** set includes the rare and the advanced, and the agent doesn't carry it around: it asks for it when a job actually needs it, you approve, and it goes back to normal when you close the conversation.

For the infrastructure apps we measured it: the everyday version costs the agent essentially nothing compared to before, while the complete version would have made its catalogue 48% heavier.
