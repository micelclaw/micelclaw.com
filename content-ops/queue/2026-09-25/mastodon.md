How do you find a contact when you remember the job, not the name?

In Micelclaw Contacts, ask "who wires our shows?" The agent returns the person as a clickable contact chip. Tap it for their card; tell the agent a new number and it updates the card.

The 39-second tour shows the actual screen. Contacts and agent run on your own machine.
