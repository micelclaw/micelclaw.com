This is our home server. We just asked it to plan our week.

*(title card — silencio)*

Micelclaw is a self-hosted AI productivity OS.
Notes, mail, calendar, photos, files, money — one local database, on your own hardware.

On top, seven agents that actually know your data — on the web, on Telegram, even by voice.

Ask for a plan. You get a real document, rendered as it's written.

Hard question? The agents deliberate. You get to watch.

Tell your agent to save a note.
Open Notes. It's already there.

Everything you keep becomes part of one graph.

Photos you can search by what's in them.
Files with instant previews. Share with a QR.
Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal. One inbox.
And real accounting. Taxes included.

All of this runs on a mini-PC. Local models if you want.
Your data never leaves home.

*(outro)*

Micelclaw. Your self-hosted AI productivity OS.
