Sunday is the make-up date for the rained-out Valencia beach show — and the whole road to it
lives on one calendar. Four color-coded calendars in one view, click-to-create with reminders,
and direct manipulation: drag an event to move it, stretch it to make room, drop it on another day.

Then the part that changes how you plan: you don't open the calendar at all. Ask your agent what
the week looks like and it reads it back — every event a clickable chip. Say "put the final
rehearsal on Friday evening" and it's scheduled, on the right calendar, reminder set. Change of
plans? Say so. The chip is the door: click it and you're standing on the event.

Everything here is the live product running on a mini-PC at home. No demo reels.
