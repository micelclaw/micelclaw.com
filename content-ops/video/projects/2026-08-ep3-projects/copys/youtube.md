**Title:** Micelclaw OS Ep. 3 — Projects: boards that move from chat

**Description:**
The Valencia beach show has stopped being a pile of follow-ups. The work now lives on one board:
Backlog, Ready, In Progress, Review and Done, with priorities, dates, checklists, dependencies and
linked records visible where the team needs them.

The same data can become a table, a Gantt plan, a calendar or a dashboard. Then we leave Projects
entirely. From the chat, we ask Francis to create a high-priority `Print run of show` card in Ready,
due Saturday, with three checklist items. The tool calls stay visible, the new card comes back as a
chip, and one click opens the exact card on the exact board.

Projects also links the plan to the rain-contingency note and the supplier behind the show. That is
where the next tour begins: Contacts.

Everything here is the live product running on a mini-PC at home. No demo reels.
